## Signal 1 — the `BUDGET` line: three claims checked, three corrections

1. **"First `BUDGET` line ever" — false.** `handoff.sh grep "BUDGET|STALL"` returns **three** in this repo's own memory: 2026-09-13 (x2) and 2026-09-18. Worse, two are written in **turns** and one in **minutes**, so no trend can be computed across them.
2. **The turns→minutes conversion was correct, and the field is the defect.** `200 turns × 47 s = 157 min` is the conversion §8 mandates, so the agent did the right thing. But the field carries no unit: **58 of 61** task files have a bare `**Budget**: N` (3 carry `min`), and the bare values are the turn-era table (60/120/200), not the minute table (45/90/150/240). Two agents read the same bare `200` differently; the other reading turned a 60 % overrun into a clean report.
3. **`elapsed` is not a measure of the agent.** The line declares 238 min vs 157 and its own subtraction removes ~99 as a suspended machine — leaving **~140 worked, inside budget**. The line announcing the overrun disproves it. And the 99 min (two consecutive commits, 01:34:05 → 03:13:21) is, by §8's own definition of a stall, **indistinguishable from a stall**; only the agent's word separates them, and that word is what the instrumentation exists to replace.

**Root cause of (2), found and fixed.** `.claude/skills/quickfix/SKILL.md:24` still emitted `**Budget**: 60 | 120 | 200 ← turns from the estimate` while `implementation-planner.md` and `implementing-common.md §8` had moved to minutes. All three now emit the unit **inside the value** (`90 min`), so the comment that was being dropped on copy no longer carries the information.
